Refined Process — Summary

The merchant journey runs through four portals — Website, Operations Dashboard, Compliance Portal, and the Merchant Dashboard / Mobile App — with a continuous read/write link to the Internal Dashboard for monitoring, support and downstream approvals.

1 Website signup

Prospect submits Owner KYC (Ghana Card), business details, registration docs & Directors' IDs if a registered company, and a PEP declaration with expected monthly receive amount.

2 Operations triage

The application lands in the New Applications queue. The Team Lead assigns it to an Account Manager, who reviews completeness. Missing info bounces back to the merchant; complete applications are forwarded to Compliance and a Compliance ID is generated.

3 Compliance review

Compliance reviews every section and issues one of four outcomes: Approved, Flagged (approved with monitoring), Request for Info, or Rejected. RFI and Rejected return to Operations, who re-engages the merchant. Approved/Flagged moves to Pending Setup.

4 Business account setup

Account Manager generates the Business ID, configures the business profile, branches, services, settlement (Bank/Momo), and assigns a USSD prefix + extension from the internal pool (uniqueness enforced). On completion the merchant flips to Active in Operations and is mirrored in the Compliance Portal's Merchants section.

5 Merchant login & live operations

Logins are provisioned for the Owner and Employees using email or phone, authenticated by OTP. The Merchant Dashboard provides transactions, transfers, CRM with customer loyalty, role management, support, KYC, branches, services and USSD. The Merchant Mobile App is available to Approved and Flagged merchants for in-person collections.

6 Internal sync & ongoing approvals

  • Payments & transactions — mirror to Internal Dashboard.
  • Support requests — handled by internal Support; threaded back to merchant.
  • KYC changes — go to Operations first, then Compliance for re-review.
  • Branch requests — Operations review & approve.
  • Service added — no approval needed; reflected internally.
  • USSD added — system assigns unique prefix + extension from the central pool.
Dual-ID rule. Each merchant carries two separate identifiers that map 1:1 to the same master record.
CMP-XXXXXX — Compliance ID, minted at first compliance handoff. Lives in the Compliance Portal across the merchant's lifetime.
BIZ-XXXXXX — Business ID, minted at account setup. Used by Operations, Merchant Dashboard, Mobile App and Internal Dashboard.
Either ID resolves to the same merchant in every portal.

KYC Back-and-Forth Rule

Any KYC change initiated by the merchant — whether during onboarding or after going live — must pass through the Operations Dashboard first. The Account Manager validates the submission, then forwards to Compliance for the final review and decision. Compliance never receives KYC updates directly from the merchant.
